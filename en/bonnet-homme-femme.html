Men's or women's beanie? A beanie for everyone

Do you need a men's or women's beanie? Why most beanies are unisex, and how to choose the one that suits you.

In shops, beanies are often sorted into men's and women's sections. Yet a beanie doesn't really have a gender. What matters is the shape, the size and a style that feels like you.

A beanie is unisex

A beanie covers your head and ears, whoever wears it. So-called men's and women's beanies mostly differ in colour and the way they're presented, rarely in their cut.

What really changes: size

The real difference is head circumference, which varies by a few centimetres from one person to another. Our beanies are one size and fit most adult heads. For an exact fit, the customisable beanie is made to your head size.

Choose by style

Bright or neutral, leopard or floral, thick cuff: pick what you like, not what's expected of you. In our photos, Joon and Paco wear the same beanies, and that's on purpose.

An easy gift

Since a beanie suits everyone, it's a simple gift, with no risk of picking the wrong section. Just choose colours the person loves.
