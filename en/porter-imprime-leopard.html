How to wear leopard print in winter

Leopard is no longer just for the daring. Our tips for wearing it in winter without overdoing it, starting with a beanie.

Leopard print comes back every winter, and it's never been easier to wear. The secret: get the dose right. Here's how to make it part of your everyday look.

One piece at a time

The simplest rule: one leopard item per outfit. A beanie, a scarf or a bag is enough to add character. Keep the rest simple: black, grey, beige, denim.

Start with an accessory

If you've never dared to wear leopard, start with a beanie. It stands out without taking over your outfit, and you can take it off in a second. It's the easiest way to try the print.

Leopard in colour

Leopard isn't just beige and black anymore. It comes in pink, blue, grey and khaki. These colourful versions are softer and match a jacket or coat in the same shade.

Leopard and bright colours

For the bolder, leopard pairs really well with a strong colour: a pink, forest green or turquoise cuff makes the pattern pop. That's what we love doing on our beanies.

Find your leopard beanie

Many of our beanies come in leopard, in different colours. Each one is one of a kind. And if you'd rather pick the combination yourself, design your beanie with our fabrics.
