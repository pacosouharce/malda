A one-of-a-kind beanie, the gift that always lands

No need to know their size or exact taste: a handmade, one-of-a-kind beanie is the simple gift that always pleases.

Finding a gift that truly pleases, without it ending up in a cupboard, is the puzzle of every holiday season. A beanie ticks a lot of boxes.

It's useful straight away

Given in winter, a beanie gets worn the very next day. It's a useful gift that people keep on them, and they think of whoever gave it every time they head out into the cold.

No size headache

Our beanies are one size and fit most adult heads. No need to secretly ask for a shoe or clothing size. And if you want to be sure, the customisable beanie is made to the exact head size.

A gift nobody else will have

Every MALDA beanie is one of a kind, with its own colours and its own name. Whoever receives it knows they won't see the same one in the street. That small detail is what sets it apart from a chain-store gift.

A gift that means something

Our beanies are upcycled and handmade in Annecy by two people. Giving a MALDA beanie also supports a small workshop and a kind of fashion that gives textiles a second life.

How to give it

Pick the beanie on the site, then find it on Vinted to buy it. Order early enough before the holidays so it arrives in time. And if you can't decide, design it yourself with the customisable beanie.
