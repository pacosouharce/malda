Which beanie for a winter in Annecy?

Cold wind on the lake, days out on the Semnoz, freezing mornings in town: our advice on choosing a beanie for an Annecy winter.

Winter in Annecy means a lake that steams on the coldest mornings, a bise wind that cuts through town and snowy peaks twenty minutes away by car. A good beanie is no small detail here.

Cover your ears properly

That's where you feel the cold first, especially by the lake when the wind picks up. A beanie with a turn-up doubles the thickness right over your ears. Check that it comes down far enough without having to pull on it.

A material that keeps you warm

Wool, fleece, blends: each MALDA beanie's material is listed in its ad. Wool stays warm even when a little damp, which helps in the snow. Fleece is light, soft and dries quickly after a snowshoe climb.

One beanie for every outing

The same beanie should work for a walk on the Pâquier, a trip up the Semnoz or an afternoon at the Col de la Forclaz. We prefer a simple model that slips into a pocket or a bag over a technical beanie you only wear in the mountains.

What about size?

Our beanies are one size, designed to fit most adult heads. The measurement is given in each Vinted listing. If in doubt, ask directly on the listing, we reply quickly.
