What to do in Annecy in winter? Our ideas

Misty lake, Christmas market, snowshoeing on the Semnoz, hot chocolate in the old town: our ideas for enjoying Annecy when it's cold.

In winter, Annecy changes face. Fewer people, a lake steaming at dawn, white peaks right above the town. Here are the outings we love when it's cold, beanie on.

The Pâquier at sunrise

Early in the morning, mist floats over the lake and the mountains slowly emerge from the clouds. A walk across the Pâquier and along the Thiou is the best way to start a winter day.

The old town and the Christmas market

In December, the old town lights up and the Christmas market sets up. You wander between the canals, sip mulled wine or hot chocolate, and find gift ideas made by local craftspeople.

Snowshoes and sledging on the Semnoz

Half an hour from town, the Semnoz offers snowshoe walks with views over the lake and Mont Blanc. For younger ones, the sledging slopes are always a hit.

The Col de la Forclaz and the views

On a clear day, head up to the Col de la Forclaz to see the whole lake at a glance. Dress warmly: up there the wind is unforgiving, and a beanie that covers your ears becomes essential.

Gear up
