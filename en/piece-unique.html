One of a kind: why no two MALDA beanies are alike

Patterns, colours, tiny batches: why each MALDA beanie exists only once.

On our site, each beanie has its own photo, its own name and its own listing. That isn't a display choice: each one exists in a single copy.

One textile, one beanie

Because we work with upcycling, we start from textiles that already have a history. Their patterns, colours and checks are all different. Two beanies may look alike, but they will never be identical.

Very small batches

Everything is handmade, by the two of us, in Annecy. We don't build up stock: beanies arrive as we go, a few at a time. If one catches your eye, better not to wait too long.

Your opinion counts

When you arrive on the site, we ask which of four beanies you prefer. Your answers help us see which colours and patterns you like most, so we can choose the next textiles.
