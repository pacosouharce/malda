Which beanie size should you choose? Measuring your head

Too tight and a beanie gives you a headache; too big and it falls over your eyes. How to measure your head in a minute and pick the right size.

A beanie that's too tight leaves a mark on your forehead and ends up giving you a headache. Too big, it slides over your eyes or flies off in the first gust of wind. Good news: finding your size takes a minute.

Measuring your head

Take a soft tape measure. Wrap it around your head just above your eyebrows and ears, where a beanie sits. Keep the tape flat, without pulling. No tape measure? A piece of string works: mark it, then measure it with a ruler.

Common sizes

Most adults have a head circumference between 54 and 60 cm. Below 55 cm is usually considered a small head, above 59 cm a large one. Children are generally between 48 and 54 cm.

One size: who is it for?

Our beanies are one size and fit most adult heads. The cuff and the soft fabrics give a little flexibility. The exact measurement is listed in each Vinted listing.

For a made-to-measure beanie

If your head is smaller or larger than average, the customisable beanie is for you. You choose your fabrics, enter your head size to the centimetre, and we make it to fit.
