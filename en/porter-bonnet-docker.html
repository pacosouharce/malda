How to wear a docker beanie (the short beanie)

The docker beanie, short and sitting above the ears, is everywhere this winter. Our tips for wearing it well, whatever your style.

The docker beanie, also called a short beanie or fisherman beanie, stops above the ears and is worn with a well-defined cuff. Born on the docks, it has become a winter fashion classic. It's the shape of our MALDA beanies.

Where to place it

A docker beanie sits high on the head, just above the ears, or halfway over them if you're cold. It doesn't come down over the forehead: it shows a bit of hair and keeps your face clear.

Straight or slightly tilted

Worn straight, it looks neat and a little nautical. Tilted slightly back or to the side, it gets more relaxed. Try both in front of a mirror: a few centimetres change everything.

What to wear it with

It goes with almost everything: a work jacket, a puffer, a long coat or a fleece. Since our beanies have bold colours and patterns, they work really well with a simple outfit, letting the beanie be the statement piece.

Long or short hair

With long hair, let it fall over your shoulders and wear the beanie high. With short hair, wear it a little lower to cover the top of your ears. With a fringe, place the cuff just above it.

A good docker beanie

A good short beanie stays on without squeezing and doesn't ride up by itself. The cuff should be thick enough to keep its shape. Our beanies are made for that, and each one is one of a kind.
